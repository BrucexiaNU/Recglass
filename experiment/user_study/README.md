# User Study

## Participants

A total of 14 participants completed the study.

All participants completed both the robot-control evaluation and the health-sensing evaluation prior to the questionnaire.

---

## Questionnaire Design

Participants rated their agreement with a set of statements using a 5-point Likert scale:

1 = Strongly Disagree
2 = Disagree
3 = Neutral
4 = Agree
5 = Strongly Agree

The questionnaire evaluated five dimensions:

- Comfort： I felt comfortable wearing the system during the evaluation.
- Ease of Use：The system was easy to learn and operate.
- Intuitiveness：The interaction methods felt intuitive.
- Flexibility：The platform could be easily adapted to different sensing tasks.
- Overall Experience： I was satisfied with the overall experience of using the system.

---

## User Study Results

| Metric | Mean Score |
|----------|----------|
| Comfort | 4.2 |
| Ease of Use | 4.5 |
| Intuitiveness | 4.6 |
| Flexibility | 4.8 |
| Overall Experience | 4.5 |

---

## Extended Wearability Study

Three participants volunteered for an extended deployment study.

Each participant wore the system for more than 5 hours per day over three consecutive days while performing daily activities and experimental tasks.

After the deployment period, participants completed the same questionnaire again.

Average scores remained above 4.0 across all evaluation dimensions, indicating that prolonged use did not substantially reduce user acceptance or satisfaction.

---

## Qualitative Feedback

Participants consistently highlighted the importance of the platform's reconfigurability.

Common feedback themes included:

- Ability to reposition sensors for different sensing tasks.
- Ability to adjust sensor placement for different facial structures.
- Ability to remove unnecessary sensing modules to reduce weight and improve comfort.
- Flexibility to support multiple sensing applications using the same wearable platform.

These observations suggest that reconfigurability is an important contributor to both usability and long-term wearability.
